Soft gray-jade water with sparse long ripples indicating upward travel, no glitter noise. Crisp clear cartoon pixel-art,48 or fewer coherent colors, restrained warm-gray/blue-green, larger clusters, no photorealistic texture. Designed for readable decorative boat sprites and three-lane escort gameplay; shore detail only at edges. No UI, lettering, logos, frame or gradients.
